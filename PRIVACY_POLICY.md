# Privacy Policy

**Familje Dashboard**  
Last Updated: June 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Familje Dashboard ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number, password
- **Family Information**: Names, birthdates, relationships of family members
- **Profile Data**: Photos, biographical information, preferences
- **Content**: Meal plans, shopping lists, budget information, diary entries, tasks, reminders, goals, and notes
- **Payment Information**: Billing address, payment method (if applicable)
- **Communication**: Messages, feedback, support requests

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Features accessed, content viewed, actions taken, time spent
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates (only with your permission)
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: Session identifiers, preferences
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, pages visited, referring URLs

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Weather Services**: Location-based weather data from Open-Meteo API
- **Authentication Providers**: If you use OAuth/social login
- **Analytics Providers**: Aggregated usage statistics

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

- **Service Provision**: Delivering core functionality (meal planning, budgeting, task management)
- **Personalization**: Customizing your experience based on preferences
- **Communication**: Sending notifications, reminders, support messages
- **Improvement**: Analyzing usage patterns to improve features
- **Security**: Detecting fraud, preventing abuse, enforcing terms
- **Legal Compliance**: Fulfilling legal obligations
- **Analytics**: Understanding user behavior and trends

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Data Storage

- **Location**: Data is stored on secure servers in the European Union
- **Database**: MySQL/TiDB with encryption at rest
- **Backups**: Regular encrypted backups maintained
- **Retention**: Data retained as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion

### 4.2 Security Measures

- **Encryption**: TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit
- **Authentication**: OAuth 2.0 with secure session management
- **Access Control**: Role-based access (admin/user)
- **Monitoring**: Continuous security monitoring and logging
- **Compliance**: GDPR and data protection best practices

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 We Do NOT Share Your Data With

- Third-party advertisers
- Data brokers or marketing companies
- Unauthorized third parties

### 5.2 We MAY Share Your Data With

- **Service Providers**: Cloud hosting, analytics, payment processors (under strict agreements)
- **Legal Requirements**: Law enforcement, court orders, legal obligations
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or bankruptcy
- **Family Members**: Only information you explicitly share within the app

### 5.3 Your Family's Privacy

- **Shared Data**: Information you add about family members is visible to other family members in your account
- **Control**: You control what information is shared within your family group
- **Children**: Parents/guardians are responsible for children's data

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 GDPR Rights (EU Residents)

- **Access**: Right to access your personal data
- **Rectification**: Right to correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Right to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction**: Right to restrict processing
- **Portability**: Right to receive data in portable format
- **Objection**: Right to object to processing

### 6.2 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

- **Disclosure**: Right to know what data is collected
- **Deletion**: Right to delete personal information
- **Opt-Out**: Right to opt-out of data sales
- **Non-Discrimination**: Right to equal service despite privacy choices

### 6.3 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@familjedashboard.app with:
- Your account email
- Specific request (access, correction, deletion, etc.)
- Proof of identity

We will respond within 30 days.
